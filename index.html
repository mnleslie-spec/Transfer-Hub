Need Help?

You don't have to figure out the transfer process alone.

If you're unsure about your next step, consider reaching out to the appropriate Madison College support office or your academic advisor.

  • Talk with your academic advisor about your academic plan.
  • Research universities early so you know their requirements.
  • Keep a personal list of deadlines and scholarship opportunities.
  • Ask questions when you're unsure about credits or requirements.
